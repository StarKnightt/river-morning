# Credits

Every third-party asset and every piece of borrowed code in River Morning, with its source, author and licence. This
file ships with the game (as `CREDITS.md` next to the game's page in every build, linked from the pause menu). It
brings together the project's earlier `CREDITS.md` and the credits of every patch: fishing, fish, time of day, sound,
character, camp, forest, water, the scene polish and the final pass (fishing and fish polish, rod down, grass,
distance clarity, the walk-in cabin, robustness and quality presets). Reference photos the critic used are never
shipped; they are listed at the end, followed by the release check list. The game's own code is MIT-licensed
(`LICENSE` in https://github.com/StarKnightt/river-morning); everything listed here keeps its own licence.

All Poly Haven assets are CC0 1.0 (public domain, https://polyhaven.com/license); they are credited anyway.

No third-party source code was copied into the game; everything else is River Morning's own code. These techniques
were studied and written anew; the licences of the studied code were checked against each project's LICENSE file or
source headers (all MIT or BSD-3-Clause):

- **Water (part 2):** the ribbon mesh, flow and foam bake and two-phase flow map after Arnklit's Waterways (MIT) and
  CatLikeCoding's FlowUVW; equal-curvature caustic waves and clear-water optics after ScottieFox's caustic-volume
  (MIT); differential-area caustics after Evan Wallace's webgl-water (MIT), solved analytically per pixel;
  screen-space refraction with foreground rejection after three's `webgpu_backdrop_water` example (MIT); Lengyel's
  oblique near-plane clipping (as in three's ReflectorNode, MIT), extended to reversed depth. Also studied:
  martinRenou/threejs-caustics (BSD-3-Clause), sydneyzh/threejs-water and jeantimex/threejs-water (MIT),
  norio's three-stylized-water (MIT), VictorZakharov's beautiful-water (MIT).
- **Forest, rocks and mist (part 3):** coverage-preserving (mip-scaled) alpha test after Ben Golus' article
  "Anti-aliased Alpha Test"; the grass's rooted bend with travelling gust fronts after the meadow-grass example of the
  threejs-procedural-vegetation skill (scottstts, MIT; studied, not copied); the mist samples three's own shadow map
  with three's shadow-coordinate convention (`lightShadowMatrix`, MIT).
- **Skies (part 8):** the Radiance (.hdr) decoder is our own, following the Radiance format and three's HDRLoader
  conventions.
- **Distance clarity (final pass):** `sharpen()` follows the published algorithm of AMD FidelityFX CAS (MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2020 Advanced Micro Devices, Inc.); the TSL code is written for River Morning, no source copied.

The rest of the final pass (fishing and fish polish, rod down, grass, the walk-in cabin, robustness, quality
presets) adds no third-party assets or code.
